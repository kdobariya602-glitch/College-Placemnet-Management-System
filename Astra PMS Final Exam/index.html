The Journey

Placement Process

Eight checkpoints from registration to your offer letter.

01
Student Registration

Register on the TPC portal with academic and personal details.

02
Resume Verification

Coordinators verify and approve your resume before drives.

03
Training Sessions

Attend mandatory aptitude, coding and soft-skill training.

04
Aptitude Test

Clear the recruiter's shortlisting aptitude round.

05
Technical Interview

Domain interview with the recruiting panel.

06
HR Interview

Final round assessing fit, communication and intent.

07
Offer Letter

Receive and review the official offer letter.

08
Successful Placement

Confirm acceptance and begin onboarding formalities.
