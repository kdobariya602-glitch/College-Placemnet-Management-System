Rules of Engagement

Placement Policies

Clear, consistent rules that keep the placement process fair for every student and every recruiter.

01
Eligibility Criteria
  • Minimum 60% aggregate or 6.5 CGPA across semesters
  • No active academic backlog at the time of a drive
  • Maximum of two backlogs cleared historically per policy year
02
Attendance Rules
  • 75% minimum attendance in placement training sessions
  • Two consecutive unexcused absences forfeit drive eligibility for that cycle
  • Medical exemptions require prior written approval
03
Offer Acceptance Policy
  • One offer acceptance per student per placement cycle
  • Accepted offers are binding once confirmed on the portal
  • Withdrawal after acceptance requires TPC Director approval
04
Dream Company Policy
  • Students with a standard offer may still sit for a pre-approved 'dream' company
  • Dream company package threshold is revised and published every year
  • Only one dream company attempt permitted per student
05
Internship Rules
  • Internship offers are logged separately from full-time placements
  • Minimum 8-week duration to count toward degree requirements
  • Conversion offers follow the same acceptance policy as placements
06
Registration Guidelines
  • Complete profile verification is mandatory before applying to any drive
  • Resume must be approved by the placement coordinator
  • False information on the portal leads to disqualification
07
Dress Code
  • Formal business attire is mandatory for all placement activities
  • Institutional ID card must be visible during every drive
  • Casual wear is not permitted on interview or test days
08
Code of Conduct
  • Punctuality and professionalism are expected at every stage
  • Any form of malpractice results in immediate disqualification
  • Respectful communication with recruiters is mandatory at all times

Important Notes

  • Policies are reviewed and updated at the start of every academic year by the Placement Advisory Committee.
  • In case of conflict, the version published on the official portal supersedes any printed copy.
  • Exceptions to any policy require written approval from the Placement Director.
